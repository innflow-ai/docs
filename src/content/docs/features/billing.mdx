## Plans

Innflow plans are credit-based. Credits are included with each plan and refresh on the plan's billing cycle.

| Plan family | Starting price | Included credits | Best for |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|----------|
| **Free** | $0/mo | See the allowance in your account | Exploring workflows and basic automation |
| **Pro** | $19.99/mo | 10,000 monthly credits | Individual automation with higher usage |
| **Business** | $199.99/mo | 140,000 monthly credits | Teams with higher usage |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Dedicated support, custom limits, and advanced requirements |

Prices are in USD. Annual commitments are billed monthly at a lower rate: $16.99 for Pro or $169.99 for Business. Review the [pricing page](https://innflow.ai/pricing) and the terms shown at checkout before choosing a plan.

## Credit Allowances

Pro and Business each include a monthly credit allowance. Your billing settings show the allowance and remaining balance for your account. Enterprise allowances are agreed with the team.

## Usage Credits

Each workflow execution consumes credits based on the nodes it runs. AI, external API calls, research, code execution, and advanced integrations typically cost more than basic trigger or utility nodes.

Your credit balance is visible in the app sidebar and billing settings.

### Low Balance Warnings

- **Low balance** — You are approaching your monthly limit
- **Critical balance** — You have very little credit remaining and workflows may stop executing

## Managing Your Subscription

Navigate to **Settings → Billing** to:

- View your current plan and credit usage
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Manage annual or monthly billing
- View transaction history showing what each execution cost
