---
title: "Social Publishing"
description: "Publish, draft, or schedule content across social and messaging channels"
---

Innflow includes publishing nodes for social networks and messaging channels. Connect an approval step before a publishing node when a person should review generated content.

## Available channels

| Node | Capability | Connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | Share a post or retrieve profile data | LinkedIn OAuth |
| **Meta** | Publish a Facebook Page post or Instagram image post | Meta connection |
| **X/Twitter** | Create, draft, or schedule a post | X OAuth |
| **Reddit** | Create, draft, or schedule a post | Connected publishing account |
| **Bluesky** | Create, draft, or schedule a post | Connected publishing account |
| **Threads** | Create, draft, or schedule a post | Connected publishing account |
| **Telegram Post** | Create, draft, or schedule a Telegram channel post | Connected publishing account |
| **Pinterest** | Create, draft, or schedule a pin | Connected publishing account |
| **WhatsApp** | Send an approved WhatsApp template message | WhatsApp connection |

<Callout type="warning">
  Publishing operations can create public or customer-facing content. Test with a non-production account and use [Human Approval](/tools/logic-utility#human-approval) when review is required.
</Callout>

## Draft and schedule behavior

Publishing nodes can expose draft or scheduling choices when supported by the selected channel. The destination account and operation are configured in the node; downstream output includes the provider response or published item details.

Account capabilities differ by provider. For example, Meta publishing requires an eligible Facebook Page or Instagram account, and WhatsApp sends an existing approved message template rather than arbitrary free-form text.
