## Make information retrievable

A knowledge base organizes source material for retrieval. It is useful when a workflow needs relevant passages from a larger collection rather than one fixed prompt containing everything.

## Prepare the source material

Add the documents your process needs and check their processing state. Clear, current source material is more useful than a large collection of duplicates or obsolete instructions.

## Inspect retrieval before generation

Test a question whose answer you know exists in the material. Inspect the returned context before adding a generation step. Then test a question the documents cannot answer and decide how the workflow should handle missing evidence.

## Try it

Use a short FAQ document. Ask one supported question and one unrelated question. Configure the process to acknowledge missing information instead of treating every answer as supported.
