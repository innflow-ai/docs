---
title: "Account and Workspace Settings"
description: "Manage your profile, appearance, workspace identity, and members"
---

Open **Settings** from the app sidebar to manage your personal account and the active workspace.

## Profile

The **General** page shows your profile photo, name, and account email.

- Select your avatar to upload a new profile photo.
- Select the edit icon beside your name to change it.
- Your email identifies the signed-in account and is shown as read-only on this page.

## Appearance

Open **Personalization** to choose how Innflow displays the workspace:

- **Light** always uses the light theme.
- **Dark** always uses the dark theme.
- **System** follows your operating system or browser preference.

Select **Save** after changing the color mode. The preference applies across the workspace interface.

## Workspace

Open **Workspace** to view the active workspace, its logo, member count, included seats, and your access level.

Workspace admins can:

- Upload or replace the workspace logo.
- Rename the workspace.
- Invite members while seats are available.

Members can view the workspace roster but cannot change admin-controlled settings. If you are using a personal workspace, create or switch to a team workspace before configuring shared settings.

See [Teams and Workspaces](/features/teams) for workspace limits, invitations, and roles.
